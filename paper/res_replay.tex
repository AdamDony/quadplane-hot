The dataset~\cite{Dony2026data} records the aircraft's own hand-offs under the threshold rule, including a front transition in which the rotors were cut early and the wing lost lift for three seconds, and a back transition that ran to a high angle of attack. Fig.~\ref{fig:replay} starts HOT from the state recorded \SI{2.7}{s} before that rotor cut, at \SI{5.4}{m/s} and climbing, and from the state recorded as the back transition began, at \SI{13.4}{m/s} with the rotors off. Both states lie outside the training distribution of trims. From the front state HOT keeps the lift fraction above \hotReplayFrontLf{} where the recorded flight lost its lift, but the module holds the rotors through \hotReplayFrontInterv{} certified interventions in the low-speed climb, where the plant set is least contracted, and cruise is not reached within the \SI{20}{s} window; from the back state it spins the rotors up within the step bound, keeps the angle of attack below \hotReplayBackAl{}$^\circ$, where the recorded flight went to $36^\circ$, and reaches the band in \hotReplayBackT{}~s with \hotReplayBackInterv{} interventions.
