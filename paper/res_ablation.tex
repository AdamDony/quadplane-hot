Fig.~\ref{fig:ablation} removes one module at a time and reduces the window length, the width and the depth, each variant trained with the same procedure (500 iterations for the reduced architectures) and evaluated on the \numAblPlants{} plants of the cross-plant cell, bare and with the module. Without the physics features the time to target rises to \ablNoPhysT{}~s and the violation rate of the bare policy to \ablNoPhysViol{}\%; without the auxiliary head the context token loses its plant estimate ($R^2$ from \interpRtwoMean{} to \ablNoAuxRtwo{}) and the time to target rises to \ablNoAuxT{}~s; the feed-forward policy on the current state, which has no history to infer the plant from, violates on \mlpCrossViol{}\% of the samples, and the recurrent policy on \lstmCrossViol{}\%. A window of 8 samples, a width of 32 and a single block are compared with the full model of 32 samples, width 48 and two blocks.
